Ruqayyah brings the book "Al-Ruqyah Al-Shar'iyyah from the Holy Quran and the Prophetic Sunnah" by Dr. Khalid bin Abdulrahman Al-Juraisi to your phone. It works fully offline.

<b>Content</b>
- Etiquette and general guidelines to observe when performing Ruqyah
- Short Ruqyah
- Medium Ruqyah
- Long Ruqyah

<b>Features</b>
- Counter for each dhikr, with optional vibration and sound at every count, at the end of a dhikr, or when all are done
- Count using the volume keys
- Resume from where you stopped last time
- Filter adhkar by source (Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, the Sunan...) and by grading (authentic, good, weak...)
- Font size and font type settings, with an option to show or hide diacritics
- Keep the screen on while reading
- Arabic interface

Free, ad-free and open source. Part of Muslim Pack.
